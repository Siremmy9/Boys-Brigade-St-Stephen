ABOUT US
The Boys' Brigade is an International, Interdenominational Christian Uniform Youth Organization that trains Boys to become Reliable Citizens of ones Country

OBJECT. PROMISE. PILLAR.

The object of the Boys' Brigade is the Advancement of Christ kingdom, among boys, and the Promotion of Habits, of Obedience, Reverence, Self-Respect and all that tends towards a true Christian's Manliness

Sure & Stedfast (Hebrew 6:19)

The Boys' Brigade Promise

On my honour, I Promise to carry-out my duties, in accordance
with the constitution and the regulation of the brigade
and to undertake all necessary trainings,
at the earliest opportunity, so help me God

Boys Brigade Prayer

Bless o God the Boys Brigade, and give to it greater power
to advance thy kingdom, throughout the world,
grant that in thy mercies that every members
past and present may prove steadfast in the fight against evil
and truth in alligiance . help us in times of temptation,
make us strong when we are weak, give us courage
in difficulties, faithfulness in duties, loyalty in friendship
and finally by your mercies, bring us into your everlasting kingdom,
through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen
